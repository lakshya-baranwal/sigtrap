The STAR method is a structured technique for responding to behavioral interview questions by describing a specific situation, task, action, and result.

### The STAR Framework:
- **Situation (S)**: Set the scene and provide context (What was the project/issue?).
- **Task (T)**: Describe your responsibility and challenge in that situation.
- **Action (A)**: Explain exactly what steps you took to address the challenge.
- **Result (R)**: Share the outcomes of your actions, using metrics and data where possible.
